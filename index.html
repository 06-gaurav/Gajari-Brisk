About Us

Engineering Tomorrow's Hybrid Mobility

Founded at MNNIT Allahabad by young automotive engineers, Gajari Brisk Private Limited is committed to building modular, scalable, and sustainable hybrid solutions tailored for Indian operating environments.

Vision

To make clean, intelligent commercial transport accessible to every business across India.

Mission

Deliver hybrid platforms that combine electric efficiency with dependable range, optimized for Indian roads, fleets, and climate.

Milestones

  • 2023 Concept validation and foundational R&D for HLCCV architecture.
  • Q2 2024 Functional prototype with liquid-cooled LFP battery pack and hybrid powertrain logic.
  • Q1 2025 Extended field testing with logistics partners in North India.
  • Dec 2025 Target launch of production-ready HLCCV platform.
Technology

Hybrid Architecture Designed for Indian Missions

Our plug-in hybrid architecture combines on-board charging, engine-assisted generation, and electric traction to deliver relentless uptime without compromising efficiency.

Plug-in Hybrid / Range-Extended EV

Flexible operation modes allow direct plug-in charging, engine-generator charging, and pure electric traction when optimal.

Powertrain Modes

  • On-board plug-in charging
  • Engine-driven generator charging
  • Battery-to-motor traction (PMSM RWD / 4WD)

Battery Pack

72 V 15 kWh LFP battery with in-house liquid cooling ensuring optimal temperatures in peak Indian summers.

Engine Generator

Compact 20 kW ICE with smart motor coupling for high-efficiency range extension and continuous charging.

Intelligent Control Systems

Integrated BMS and VCU manage automatic mode switching, thermal control, and predictive energy balancing.

Lightweight Chassis

Modular tubular frame designed for strength, manufacturability, and rapid upfit for diverse payloads.
